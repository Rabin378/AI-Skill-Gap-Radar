How to read this

The numbers come from three signals. Each one is explained below.

Market value

A 0 to 100 score. It blends demand, salary, and growth, then adjusts for the 12-month change. Higher means the market pays more for the skill right now.

Your level

Your own rating from 0 to 5, taken from your skill file. The dots show the level and the amber outline marks what you have not reached yet.

  • 0Not started
  • 1Aware of it and what it is for
  • 2Learning through courses or small projects
  • 3Can apply it to real work with some help
  • 4Works with it independently
  • 5Teaches it or leads decisions about it

Coverage and gap

Coverage is the share of market value your levels already reach. The gap is the rest. A gap of 83.4% means 83.4 of every 100 points of market value are still open to you.

What drives the score

Demand counts how often a skill appears. Salary uses the midpoint of posted ranges. Growth uses the trend index and the 12-month change. Each signal is scaled to 0 to 100 before it is combined.
