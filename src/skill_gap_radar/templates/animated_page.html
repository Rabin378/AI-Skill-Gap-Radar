    How to read this

    Three signals drive the market value. Each one is scaled to 0 to 100 before it is combined.

    Market value

    Demand (45%) counts how often postings list a skill. Salary (35%) uses the midpoint of posted ranges. Growth (20%) uses the trend index and the 12-month change. The blend is then adjusted by the 12-month change.

    Coverage and gap

    Coverage is the share of market value your levels already reach. The gap is the rest. Each skill counts in proportion to its market value and to how far you still have to go.

    Learn next

    What the numbers are not

    They describe the postings you gave the tool, not every job in the market. The trend figures in the sample data are synthetic demo values.
